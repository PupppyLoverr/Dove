# Apple's Mac Dynamic Island and Spotlight: notes

Sources studied on 2026-11-06: Apple's "MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR
Display" page (apple.com/macbook-pro/proled-xdr-display), its three Dynamic
Island pictures (Spotlight, Live Activities, Siri AI); MacRumors and others
on Tahoe's Spotlight. The pictures are Apple's; they are not copied here,
only described.

Apple: "Dynamic Island is the new home for Spotlight and Live Activities.
Just like on iPhone, it's a quick way to see and interact with important
information. You can search your Mac, ask a question, track an activity, or
control your music, all in one easy-to-reach place."

## The island at rest
- A small pill of pure black, centred in the menu bar, holding the camera:
  it reads as part of the bezel. No glass, no rim: black on black at the
  top edge, rounded ends.

## Spotlight in the island
- The pill grows **sideways** into a long black capsule (about 40% of the
  screen's width, a little taller than the menu bar). The query sits in it
  in large type, its completion inline, selected:
  "tide pool**s — Open**". The top hit's icon sits at the right end.
- Below it, with a small gap, hangs a **glass panel** of the same width,
  its corners about 36 pt: a frosted blur of what's behind, its colours
  bright, a soft light rim.
- The panel's first row is **chips**: glass capsules of the apps that
  match ("Archive Utility", "Preview", "QuickTime Player", "Adobe After
  Effects").
- Then **results**, large: a 40 pt icon, the title in big white type, a
  grey line under it ("Today, 9:34 AM · 📁 Illustrations", "MPEG-4 movie ·
  736.7 MB · Today, 9:37 AM"). The chosen row is a lighter glass pill,
  with "Search Tide Pools [tab]" at its right end (Tab searches inside it).
- No category rail in the island version; the chips do the narrowing.

## Live Activities
- The island grows **down** into a black card (about 460×290 pt at the
  picture's scale), corners about 44 pt, the camera dot at its top centre.
- Inside: an icon top left (AirDrop's rings with the sender's photo), the
  title in bold ("AirDrop"), a grey subtitle ("From Aixa Rodriguez"), a
  thumbnail of the thing at the right (a PDF).
- Two capsule buttons across its foot: **Decline** (grey) and **Accept**
  (blue), each about half the width.

## Siri AI
- The island stays at rest; a tall **dark glass panel** drops below it
  (about 34% of the screen wide), corners about 40 pt, colour bleeding
  through its smoke.
- Round glass buttons at its top corners: close (×) at the left, expand
  (↗) at the right.
- Your question as a glass bubble at the right; the answer under it in
  large white text, tables and all, scrolling.

## Tahoe Spotlight (the window's own design, for our launcher's depth)
- ⌘1–4: Apps, Files, Actions, Clipboard (icons appear at the field's right
  when the pointer moves).
- Chips under the field filter (Screenshots, System Settings, Folders).
- Tab after an app's name searches inside it; "/pdf" filters by kind;
  "kind:images", "author:Tim".
- Actions: pick one, Return, fill its fields (Tab between them), Return.
- Quick keys: short phrases you assign ("ch" → ChatGPT).
- ↑ cycles past searches; holding ⌘ shows a result's path.
- Clipboard: text, links, images and files; kept 30 min, 8 h or 7 days, or
  off; Clear from its menu.

## What Keplorayx takes from this
- **The island at rest is black**, the pill blending into the screen's top
  edge; expansions that are *about the island* (a live activity, music,
  an agent asking, a timer) grow out of it in black.
- **Spotlight (Alt+Space) lives in the island:** the pill widens into the
  search capsule; results hang below in a glass panel with chips and big
  rows. Our extras live there too: agents and threads, asking with the
  answer streaming, music, plugins, widgets, actions, clipboard.
- **Ask (our Siri AI):** the island stays put and a dark glass panel drops
  below it with ×/↗ at its corners, the conversation inside: that is our
  chat with agents.
- Live activities: an agent asking (Allow blue, Deny grey), a thread done,
  music, the timer, files dropped.
