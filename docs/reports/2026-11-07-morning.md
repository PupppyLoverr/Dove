# Morning report: Keplorayx on Zeron's app and engine

Good morning. Keplorayx now runs on Zeron's whole app and engine. It's open on your screen at its intro ("Your agents, anywhere." → **Get started**), which leads into setup.

Everything is in local commits on `zgx/phase-a` (seven tonight, `777f553` → `53588e6`). Nothing is pushed. Your Zeron folder was only read. The old Keplorayx still builds as a fallback.

## What changed

**Zeron's app and engine, as Keplorayx** (`zr/`)
- Zeron's interface (`zr/apps/zgx`) and engine (`zr/crates`, `zr/apps/zeron`) are copied whole, then changed.
- The engine is `keplorayx-engine.exe`. It sits beside `keplorayx.exe`, uses its own port (27731) and keeps your data in `~/.keplorayx`, so it never touches a real Zeron install.
- **Sign-in is Woozlit**, in Zeron's sign-in screen (`src/kx_account.rs`). Zeron's account isn't used.
- **Looks:** our icons laid over Zeron's, the Orbit night and dawn colours inside Zeron's themes, and "Keplorayx" in every word, window title and the app's Windows identity.
- **Your projects** from the old app are added once, on first launch. Your old threads stay readable in the old app.
- **Agents:** every agent installed on this computer shows up (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Devin, Hermes, OpenCode). Others can be added from Zeron's registry in Settings › Providers.
- **No console window** stands behind the app.

**Setup, as Zeron's**
- The intro over the desktop, then setup **opens from the middle and expands outward** as it decodes.
- Sign-in, then the look.
- Your username and **member card**: Zeron's card design, made on this computer from Zeron's own seed, marked "keplorayx", shown as "@name on Keplorayx".
- Your organization, the agents, then "You're all set".
- The sign-in lines say what Keplorayx actually does.
- Invites and Zeron's GitHub App step are out, because both need Zeron's servers.

**The island** (`src/kx/notch.rs`, `ui/notch.luau`)
- Real glass, its pages and its live data, now read from Zeron's engine.
- **Chat in the island** works on the new engine. Tested with Zeron's mock agent: open a thread, read Zeron's own transcript, reply, allow or deny.

**Spotlight (Alt+Space)**
- After Apple's own pictures of the Mac island (notes in `docs/design/apple-island-spotlight.md`).
- At the top of the screen: your query in a **black capsule**, results in a **glass panel** below it.
- The panel holds Zeron's palette (scopes, results, keys) plus ours: apps, files, windows, clipboard, system settings, music, asking an agent.

**Widgets**
- Each widget is **glass over your wallpaper**: Windows' own copy of it, blurred and lined up with the desktop.
- Every kind works. The month calendar was fixed tonight.
- **Agents can edit them again:** the engine's own tool server offers `set_widget`, `list_widgets`, `remove_widget`, `calendar_add`, `list_calendar` and `calendar_remove`. Changes show within two seconds, and a new widget is placed for you.

**The composer:** centre, bottom and slim placements, in Settings › Appearance › New chat composer.

**Settings › General › Keplorayx:** the island, keep running in the tray, keys from any app, Start with Windows. Zeron's "Star on GitHub" banner is gone.

## Building it again

```bash
cd zr/apps/zgx && CARGO_TARGET_DIR=C:/dvw/target-kx-zgx/zr-app cargo build
cd zr && CARGO_TARGET_DIR=C:/dvw/target-kx-zgx/zr cargo build -p zeron --bin keplorayx-engine
cp C:/dvw/target-kx-zgx/zr/debug/keplorayx-engine.exe C:/dvw/target-kx-zgx/zr-app/debug/
```

Edits to `zr/apps/zgx/ui` reload in the running app without a rebuild. More detail is in `STATUS.md`.
